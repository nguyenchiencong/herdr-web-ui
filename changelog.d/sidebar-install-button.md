### Changed
- The sidebar's **Install app** button now shows only where the browser can install the app in one
  tap, such as Chrome or Edge over HTTPS, and not once the app is installed. Where there is no such
  prompt (an iPhone, Firefox, plain HTTP), the button is left out of the sidebar, and Settings →
  Phone & devices shows the steps instead. A **×** beside the button hides it on this device
  permanently; Settings still offers the install.
